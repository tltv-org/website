tltv

protocol for an open tv channel network.

watch the demo read the spec

a key.

generate an Ed25519 key pair. that's your channel. no signup, no platform, no permission needed.

a link.

your key becomes a tltv:// address on the network. share it anywhere. the protocol handles discovery, federation, and relay.

a stream.

that link resolves to standard HLS video. any browser, any player, no app required. open protocol, fully open source.
